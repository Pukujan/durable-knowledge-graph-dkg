# Citation Audit

Load this methodology when checking whether corpus statements are traceable to the intended evidence.

1. Resolve stable claim/lineage/event refs to their source artifact or snapshot refs.
2. Verify artifact integrity before trusting the citation path.
3. Verify byte/span references where the source contract supports them.
4. Preserve `verbatim` versus `reconstructed` evidence status in the audit result.
5. Flag missing, stale, ambiguous, or mismatched evidence rather than repairing citations silently.
6. Keep source quality/truth assessment separate from mechanical citation resolution.
